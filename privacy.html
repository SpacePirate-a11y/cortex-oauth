Cortex Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Overview

Cortex is a privately operated personal AI assistant. This policy describes its use of Google Calendar data.

Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, Cortex accesses Google Calendar information, including calendar names, event titles, dates, times, descriptions, and other event details.

How Information Is Used

Calendar information is used to retrieve schedules, create and manage events, and provide personal scheduling assistance.

Data Storage and Security

Cortex operates in a private environment. OAuth authentication credentials are stored locally with restricted file permissions.

Calendar information is not published through this website.

Data Sharing

Cortex is not offered as a public service. Calendar information is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Access and Revocation

The account owner can revoke Cortex's Google Calendar access through their Google Account security settings.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated if Cortex's functionality or data practices change.
