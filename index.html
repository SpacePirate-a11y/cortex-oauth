Cortex

Cortex is a privately operated personal AI assistant designed to help its owner manage everyday activities, information, and schedules.

Google Calendar Integration

Cortex connects to Google Calendar with the account owner's authorization to read, create, update, and manage calendar events.

Calendar access is used solely to provide scheduling and personal assistant functionality.

Privacy

Cortex is not a publicly accessible service. Its source code, authentication credentials, and personal data are not published through this website.
